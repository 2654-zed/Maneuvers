## B. CROSS-CHAIN / VERIFICATION INFRASTRUCTURE CHANGES

**LayerZero — policy and defaults**
- May 8 blog ("An overdue apology"): admits "we made a mistake by allowing our DVN to act as a 1/1 DVN"; since Apr 19 LZ Labs DVN rejects 1/1 configs; defaults migrated to 5/5 where feasible, minimum 3/3; Rust DVN client for diversity; RPC quorum with granular internal/dedicated/shared selection; OneSig multisig 3/5→7/10 with local tx hashing; "Console" product with automated unsafe-config detection. (`https://layerzero.network/blog/an-overdue-apology`)
- May 18 incident report (PDF): root cause = compromised LZ Labs RPC nodes (ELF implant hooking syscalls in op-geth; developer social-engineered Mar 6, EDR missed it) + DDoS on external RPCs + Kelp's 1-of-1 (previously 2-of-2, changed by app owner).
